Scott's Favorite Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1C Flour
  • 1.5t Baking Soda

Directions

  1. In med. bowl, whisk together dry ingredients.
  2. Add in milk and eggs. Whisk until just combined.
  3. Whisk in butter.
  4. Heat and butter the griddle.
  5. Ladle about ¼ cup batter and cook.

Notes

Makes about 16 pancakes.
